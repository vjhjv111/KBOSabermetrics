# Stadium materials

`clay-diff.jpg`, `clay-normal.jpg`, and `clay-rough.jpg` are derived from **Brown Mud Dry**, by **Rob Tuytel**, published by Poly Haven. The files are redistributed under **CC0 1.0 Universal** and were resized/recompressed for this game. The normal map uses OpenGL orientation; normal and roughness maps are linear data.

- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_dry
- Original file manifest: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/brown_mud_dry
- Publisher license: https://polyhaven.com/license
- CC0 legal text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode
- License verified: 2026-09-13

The turf color, normal and roughness maps, stadium geometry, crowd geometry, sky, player surface maps and uniform graphics are generated by the game code. No third-party player likeness, team logo image or stadium photograph is included in this world.
